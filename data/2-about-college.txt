Here is essential information about Lovely Professional University (LPU):

ABOUT THE UNIVERSITY:
Lovely Professional University (LPU) is India's largest single-campus university, spanning over 600+ acres on the national highway in Phagwara (near Jalandhar), Punjab. LPU is recognized globally for academic excellence, cutting-edge research, patent generation, industry collaborations, and top-tier placements. It is home to over 30,000 students from 28 Indian states and 50+ countries worldwide.

MOTTO & VISION:
- Motto: "Transforming Education, Transforming India"
- Vision: To be a premier global academic institution recognized for transformative education, cutting-edge research, innovation, and nurturing global leaders with ethical values.

RECOGNITION & ACCREDITATIONS:
- Accredited NAAC A++ (Highest grade by National Assessment and Accreditation Council with 3.68 score)
- Recognized by UGC (University Grants Commission)
- Ranked among top universities nationally and globally by NIRF and Times Higher Education (THE) World University Rankings
